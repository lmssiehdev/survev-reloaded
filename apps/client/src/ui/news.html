July 15th, 2026

Breaking the Bank

As the island aridifies, enterprising survevrs may notice that a new monetary institution has risen out of the sands: The Reserve. A massive bank constructed to hide PARMA's best kept secrets, the vault is rumoured to contain exotic and powerful weaponry.

For survevrs trudging through the desert heat, find respite, resources, and fresh water in the oasis. Or, join the hunt for the last supplies sent to the front during the Incursion, and try to find out why a familiar club's insignia keeps on appearing on these damn airdrops...

Of course, no bank heist is complete without big guns. The Department of Advanced Weaponry's newest hat trick of the S&W 500 revolver, the ASh-12 assault rifle, and the Barrett M107 anti-materiel rifle are all capable of dishing out massive damage, but be sure to conserve your precious .50 cal ammo.

May 11th, 2026

Precursor

Before the Soviet incursion and brutal fighting that ensued, PARMA scientists were given a directive: create the strongest survevr they possibly could. Their work now lies deep within the Twins...

Project: COBALT has returned! Along with much-needed adjustments for the Medic and Demo classes, PARMA is proud to announce a new perk: Indomitable Spirit, allowing survevrs to shrug off fatal injuries through (quite literally) pure adrenaline.

Additionally, the long-anticipated Survevr Pass 1 has arrived, allowing you to flex your style with over 20 different cosmetics! The pass is completely free; just log in and complete two quests per day! If you don't like a quest, you can refresh it for a new one.

As both sides exhaust their nightshade and weapon supplies, the Island rests again... what could have prompted such fierce fighting?

Over the past couple of years, PARMA has relied on third-party archives to train newbie survevrs, many of which were either misleading or hard to navigate. We've taken matters into our own hands and begun developing a comprehensive guide: the Survev.io Wiki!
